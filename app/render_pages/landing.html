More than a to-do list

Goals are easier when someone keeps you accountable.

TrackMate combines goal management with social accountability. Instead of simply ticking a task as complete, your progress can be verified by a friend you trust.

How TrackMate works

From setting a goal to having your progress verified.

01

Create a Goal

Define something meaningful you want to achieve and describe what success looks like.

02

Divide it into Tasks

Break a large goal into manageable tasks and assign each task a percentage of the total goal.

03

Connect with a Friend

Find another TrackMate user, send a friend request and build your accountability network.

04

Choose a Supervisor

Assign one of your friends to supervise your goal and review your task completion proofs.

05

Submit Proof

When you finish a task, submit photo evidence and explain what you completed.

06

Get Verified

Your supervisor approves or rejects your proof. Approval completes the task and improves your rating.
